★ Best Paper Award · ISMIR 2025

A Fourier explanation of AI-music artifacts

Darius Afchar, Gabriel Meseguer-Brocal, Kamil Akesbi, Romain Hennequin

Generative music models leave a fingerprint. We show that the deconvolution layers inside these models produce small, regularly spaced peaks in the frequency spectrum, a consequence of the architecture itself rather than the training data or weights. Those peaks make a simple, interpretable detector possible, with accuracy above 99% on tracks from generators like Suno and Udio.
