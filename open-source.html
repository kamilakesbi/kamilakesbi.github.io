02Speaker diarization

Diarizers

A library for fine-tuning speaker diarization models ("who spoke when?") on your own data, built on pyannote and the Hugging Face Hub. It comes with ready-to-use datasets, fine-tuned models and tutorial notebooks.

03Robust audio fingerprinting

deezer/musicFPaugment · Deezer · 2022–2023

Music augmentation for audio fingerprinting

AudioResearch

Apps like Shazam recognize a song from a few seconds recorded on a phone, but real recordings are messy: background chatter, traffic, room echo. To train and test recognition systems on realistic conditions, I built an open-source pipeline that simulates music recorded in noisy, real-world environments, and paired it with deep learning models that denoise the audio before recognition.

  • Adds real-world background noise (streets, cafés, transport) and room reverb to turn clean tracks into realistic recordings.
  • Used to train denoising models that make peak-based fingerprinting more robust.
  • Became my MSc thesis and an ISMIR 2023 late-breaking demo.
